Questions before you buy.

What counts as a person?

Every enabled person, administrators and break-glass accounts included. Bots, CI pipelines and AI agents do not count, and disabling someone frees their seat.

What happens when the key expires?

A paid key carries on for 30 days of grace after its expiry date (a trial key has none), time to install the new key. Then only new registrations stop: nothing that exists is cut, and audit older than 24 hours is kept, not served, until a key is installed again.

What happens if we go over our seats?

Pro carries on for 30 days of grace. Then only adding people stops: clusters, roles and the audit window stay as they are, and disabling someone frees their seat.

Does kubelatch call home?

No. The key is verified offline, so kubelatch works air-gapped, and there is no telemetry.

Can we review the code?

Yes, under a non-disclosure agreement: write to pro@kubelatch.com. The summary of an external penetration test will be published on the security page.

Is there a founders’ discount?

The first ten teams get 50% off their first year. Write to pro@kubelatch.com for the code.

Lost your key?

Have it sent again to the address you bought with.

Where are the terms?

The license and the Pro subscription terms are in Terms; how we handle your data, in Privacy.
